    Questions about the cards

    Which Bible translation is it?

    The King James Version. Its text is in the public domain in most of the world.

    Does a card cost anything?

    No. There is no account and no fee.

    How are the 1,108 verses chosen?

    We started from the verses readers mark most helpful for each need on OpenBible.info, added a full read of the Psalms, then read every one in the KJV and kept those that stand on their own.

    Can my church print the cards?

    Yes, for bulletins, slides, bookmarks and small groups.

    Is my card uploaded anywhere?

    No. The card is drawn in your browser and nothing leaves your device.

    Where do the paintings come from?

    Each verse has its own painting, made with our own model trained on public-domain Renaissance works.
