Privacy

Last updated 21 November 2026.

The cards

Every card is drawn inside your own browser. The verse, the style you pick and the image you save never leave your device, and we keep no account or profile.

Fonts

The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so your browser asks Google's servers for them. Google's own privacy policy covers that request.

Visitor counts

We count visits with Cloudflare Web Analytics. It uses no cookies and does not track you across other sites; it records things like the page visited, the referring site, the country and the browser type, so we can see how many people use the cards.

Advertising

If this site later shows ads, this page will be updated first to name the ad provider and the cookies it uses.

Questions

Leave a comment on the Verse of Grace YouTube channel and we will answer there.
